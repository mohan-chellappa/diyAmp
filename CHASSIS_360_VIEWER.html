Chassis Specifications

Amber Polycarbonate (Default View):
Warm frosted amber panels provide high contrast against the dark studio background, softly diffusing the internal polished copper chimney and corner pillars while ensuring 100% RF transparency for Wi-Fi and Bluetooth.
2× AL6061 Aluminum Finned Flanks (Left & Right):
5mm thick walls with 10 deep extruded cooling fins per side, acting as primary thermal conduction heatsinks for the three TPA3116D2 boards.
Vertical Convective Thermosiphon:
Central 35mm OD polished copper chimney + 4× 15mm corner tubes open through-and-through, creating 5 natural draft flues. Electronics chamber is dust-sealed around tubes.
External Wall-Mounted SMPS:
Mean Well LRS-350-24 hanging on the wall delivers 24V 14.6A DC via braided umbilical cable with heavy-duty GX16 4-pin aviation connector.
