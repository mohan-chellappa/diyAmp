WORKSHOP SOLDERING BEST PRACTICES
  1. Pre-Tin Wire Strands: Strip 2–3 mm of wire insulation. Twist strands tight and tin bare wire before inserting into PCB eyelets.
  2. The 3-Second Rule: Apply iron tip to pad and lead, feed 1 mm of 60/40 solder, count 1-2-3, remove heat to avoid lifting pads.
  3. Inspect for Bridges: Backlight the board and inspect with a loupe between Pads 4 & 5, 6 & 7, and 8 & 9.
  4. Strain Relief: Apply a dab of neutral RTV silicone or hot-melt glue at board wire exit edges to prevent copper fatigue.
