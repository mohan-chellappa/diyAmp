CNC & Laser Fabrication Specifications

1. Layer CUT_OUTLINE (Red, ACI 1):
Outer perimeter contour cut. Use 0.15mm beam kerf compensation on CO2 Laser (60W-100W) or 2.0mm single-flute spiral upcut carbide bit on CNC Router.
2. Layer HOLES_DRILL (Green, ACI 3):
Internal through-apertures: Central Ø35.0mm chimney flue, 4× Ø15.0mm corner tubes, GX16 4-pin (Ø16mm), 12× speaker binding posts (Ø8mm), 3.5mm AUX (Ø6mm), USB-C (10×4.2mm).
3. Layer ENGRAVE_TEXT (Cyan, ACI 4):
Surface kiss-cut / vector etching (depth 0.25mm – 0.40mm). Markings for speaker polarity (+ / -), audio channels (FL, FR, C, SL, SR, SUB), volume scale, and part identification.
4. Materials & Thicknesses:
• Front Bezel & Top Cover: 4.0mm Frosted Amber Polycarbonate.
• Rear I/O & Base Plate: 5.0mm Amber or Smoked Polycarbonate.
• Acoustic Isolation Plinth: 18.0mm Solid Walnut / Teak with R14 rounded corners.
• Side Heatsink Flanks: AL6061 Extruded Aluminum with 10 vertical cooling fins.
