**PCM1808 24bit SNR Audio Stereo ADC Single-Ended Analog-Input Decoder Amplifier Board**

The PCM1808 Audio Stereo ADC Module is a high-performance analog-to-digital converter designed for audio signal acquisition and processing applications. Featuring 24-bit resolution and a 105dB signal-to-noise ratio (SNR), it delivers precise and clean digital conversion of stereo analog audio inputs.

This module is based on the Texas Instruments PCM1808 chip, known for its excellent audio fidelity and low power consumption. It supports single-ended analog input, making it ideal for integration with various audio sources such as microphones, media players, and line-level devices.

**Featuers**

* High Resolution: 24-bit stereo ADC for accurate audio digitization
* High SNR: 105dB for clean, low-noise audio capture
* Single-Ended Input: Supports easy connection to analog audio sources
* Compact Design: Suitable for audio player projects, DIY amplifiers, and embedded systems
* Digital Output: I2S format output for compatibility with most audio processors and microcontrollers
* Low Power Operation: Efficient design ideal for portable and low-power applications

**Specifications**

* Chipset: Texas Instruments PCM1808
* Resolution: 24-bit
* SNR (Signal-to-Noise Ratio): 105 dB (typical)
* Input Type: Single-ended stereo analog
* Output Format: I2S (Inter-IC Sound)
* Sampling Frequency: 10kHz to 96kHz
* Power Supply Voltage: 3.3V or 5V DC (check module variant)
* Operating Temperature: -40°C to +85°C
