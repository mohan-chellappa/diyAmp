1. Project Overview

Pure Analog Signal Path
Zero DSP coloration or latency on audio. Pure NE5532 analog buffer, active 80–120 Hz crossover, and 5-band equalizer.
ESP32-S3 Control Only
Digital supervision strictly isolated: SPI MCP4251 digital pots for volume/EQ trim, Bluetooth A2DP receiver, and 4.3" touch display.
Hybrid Modular Wiring
Soldered header pins on all dev boards, DACs, and amp modules paired with WAGO 221 lever power distribution and JST-XH crimp disconnect harnesses. Fast servicing with zero vibration fatigue.
Natural Convection Cooling
Central 35mm OD copper chimney + 4 corner tubes draft cold air from bottom intake slots and exhaust hot air via natural thermosiphon. 0 dB fan noise.
Polycarbonate RF Architecture
Top, back, bottom, and front in shatterproof Lexan polycarbonate (εr ≈ 2.9). 100% Wi-Fi/BT transparency with naturally non-conductive rear panel.
External Wall-Mounted SMPS
Mean Well LRS-350-24 mounted on wall; feeds amp via heavy-duty GX16 4-pin umbilical (2 pins parallel per rail for 15A rating). Keeps desk tiny & isolates AC hum.

2. System Architecture & Signal Flow

Dual-ESP32-S3 Zero-Contention Architecture: ├── Controller 1 (Touch HMI / Wireless Sofa Remote): Waveshare ESP32-S3 4.3B Touch LCD (Model 28141, 800×480 IPS, 16MB Flash, 8MB PSRAM) │ ├── Dedicated 60fps LVGL Touch UI (Master Volume, 8-Zone Sliders, Crossover Freqs, EQ Presets) │ ├── Links to Audio Engine via Wireless ESP-NOW / Wi-Fi (<2ms latency, Battery Remote Mode) OR 2-Wire I2C (GPIO 8 SDA / GPIO 9 SCL) │ └── Built-in CS8501 3.7V LiPo Battery Charger + USB-C 5V + 7–36V DC Wide Input └── Controller 2 (Internal 6-Ch Audio DSP Engine): Waveshare ESP32-S3 Dev Board with Pre-Soldered Header (SKU: 1552780, 16MB Flash, 2MB PSRAM) ├── Analog Audio In (3.5mm Stereo AUX — CentIoT PJ392A-1) → PCM1808 24-Bit ADC (DIN = GPIO 4, MCLK = GPIO 0) + Bluetooth 5.0 A2DP Sink ├── Pure Digital Biquad Crossovers (>100 Hz, <100 Hz, >10 kHz) & 5-Band Parametric EQ ├── 32-bit Floating-Point Master Volume & Per-Channel Power Caps (30W / 20W / 10W) └── Multi-Lane I2S Output Bus (Shared BCK = GPIO 15, LRCK = GPIO 16, 3x Independent DOUT Lanes) ├── DOUT1 (GPIO 7) → PCM5102A DAC #1 → Amp Board #1 (FL 30W / FR 30W) ├── DOUT2 (GPIO 6) → PCM5102A DAC #2 → Amp Board #2 (Sub 30W / Mid 20W + Voice 10W) └── DOUT3 (GPIO 5) → PCM5102A DAC #3 → Amp Board #3 (Surround 10W×2 / Piezo 10W×2) └── 3x XH-M562 TPA3116D2 Mini Amps (160W Configured / 300W Peak) → 16x Binding Posts (8 Output Pairs) Power Distribution: Mains 230V AC → Mean Well LRS-350-24 (24V, 14.6A, 350W) [Wall-Mounted External] └── Umbilical (16 AWG Pair) via GX16 4-Pin Aviation Plug (2 Pins Parallel per Rail) ├── Inline 10A DC Fuse (Positive Rail Infeed) ├── WAGO 221-415 (+24V Bus Distribution Node) ├── WAGO 221-415 (0V GND Bus Distribution Node) ├── 4700µF 50V Low-ESR Bulk Reservoir Capacitor (Bus Stiffening) ├── 3x XH-M562 TPA3116D2 Mini Amp Boards (Direct 24V Rail) └── XL4015 DC-DC Buck Converter (24V → 5.0V Logic Rail) └── ESP32-S3 Audio Engine (SKU: 1552780) + 3x PCM5102A DAC Modules + PCM1808 ADC Module

5. Design Decisions Log

# Decision Chosen Rejected Reason
D1 Amp topology TPA3116D2 Class-D Class-AB Efficiency, low heat, fanless possible.
D2 Amp config 3x stereo boards 6x mono boards Same 6 channels, saves Rs 900.
D3 Power supply Mean Well LRS-350-24 Sturlite LED driver Sturlite causes audible noise; MeanWell has fan ON/OFF.
D4 Power budget 100% full capacity (350W peak) 70% clamp Mean Well LRS-350-24 (14.6A) handles full 300W audio peaks without load worries; typical listening remains at ~20W silent draw.
D5 Wiring method Soldered Header Pins + WAGO / JST-XH Loose jumper headers All module header pins soldered securely to boards; WAGO + JST-XH for modular interconnects.
D6 Enclosure AL6061, 5mm wall Plastic / steel Thermal mass + premium look.
D7 Cooling Copper chimney + fins Active fan Silent operation at all times.
D8 Transparent Panels Polycarbonate (Top, Back, Bottom, Front) Glass Polycarbonate is shatterproof, easy to CNC drill/cut, 100% RF/Wi-Fi transparent, and non-conductive for binding posts.
D9 DSP ESP32 audio firmware Dedicated DSP chip Handles BT A2DP decoding, volume attenuation, and I2S DAC clocking.
D10 Volume control ESP32 32-bit Float Digital Trim MCP4251 digital pots MCP4251 omitted / NOT required: Avoids 5V rail clipping and CMOS wiper distortion; ESP32 provides bit-perfect 32-bit logarithmic volume & per-channel trim feeding PCM5102A DACs directly.
D11 Audio Input 3.5mm Stereo AUX (CentIoT PJ392A-1) 6x RCA jacks Modern TV/Projector/BT reality; future 5.1 via HDMI ARC/Optical; saves space & ₹250.

6. Power Budget

TPA3116D2 efficiency @ 24V, 4 ohm: ~85% Peak output per stereo board: 2 x 50W = 100W 3 boards peak total output: 300W Input from PSU at 85% efficiency: 300 / 0.85 = 353W Mean Well LRS-350-24 100% Full Load Operation: PSU rating: 24V @ 14.6A = 350.4W continuous (350W peak headroom) Operating policy: 100% unconstrained — no artificial power ceiling needed Peak movie transient load: ~353W (within Mean Well overload threshold) Average listening level (75–80 dB): ~6% of max = ~18W output Average PSU input: ~21W — completely silent, zero heat buildup
