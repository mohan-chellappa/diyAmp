ESP32-C3 Super Mini Development Board Soldered Headers ESP32-C3FH4 MCU (HW-466A) A compact and powerful Wi-Fi + Bluetooth 5 (LE) development board based on the ESP32-C3 RISC-V microcontroller. Ideal for IoT, smart home, and wireless projects requiring secure, low-power connectivity. [Tutorial \[External Link\]](https://github.com/sidharthmohannair/Tutorial-ESP32-C3-Super-Mini) Troubleshooting Tips For frequent COM port disconnect issue, please hold the boot button while connecting the USB cable on the board. In Arduino IDE select the board as NoLogo ESP32C3 Super Mini Board Key Features: ESP32-C3 32-bit RISC-V CPU @ 160 MHz Built-in Wi-Fi (802.11 b/g/n) & Bluetooth 5 (LE) 4MB Flash, 400 KB SRAM (ESP32-C3FH4) USB Type-C for power and programming Breadboard-friendly super mini form factor Multiple GPIOs with ADC, PWM, SPI, I2C, UART support
