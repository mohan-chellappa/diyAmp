LEGEND & WIRING SPECIFICATIONS
  • +24V MAIN DC (Red 16 AWG): Runs from SMPS → XT60 → 10A inline fuse → WAGO 221-415 Port 1.
  • GND MAIN DC (Black 16 AWG): Runs from SMPS → XT60 → WAGO 221-415 GND Port 1.
  • +5V LOGIC BUS (24 AWG): From XL4015 Buck OUT+ → ESP32-S3 5V/VIN & 3× PCM5102A VCC.
  • I2S DIGITAL CLOCK BUS (28 AWG): Shared BCK (Bit Clock) & LRCK (Word Select) to all 3 DACs & ADC.
  • I2S DEDICATED DATA (28 AWG): DIN of DAC #1 and #3 from Pico (GPIO 7 / GPIO 6); DIN of DAC #2 from Slave ESP32-WROOM-32 (D27).
  • ANALOG AUDIO (26 AWG Shielded): 2-core shielded cable. Outer braid soldered to AGND to block RF hum.
  • SPEAKER WIRES (20–22 AWG Stranded): Directly from amp SPL+/SPL- and SPR+/SPR- to binding posts.
  • CRITICAL BTL WARNING: Speaker negative (-) terminals are Bridge-Tied Loads. DO NOT connect speaker (-) to chassis or power ground!

Step-by-Step Bench Assembly & Commissioning Guide
  1. Phase 1: Calibrate XL4015 Buck First: Before connecting ESP32 or DACs, wire 24V from SMPS into XL4015 `IN+`/`IN-`. Probe `OUT+`/`OUT-` with a digital multimeter and adjust the multiturn brass trimmer screw until output measures exactly 5.00V DC.
  2. Phase 2: Jumper PCM5102A DACs: On the bottom of each HW-280 DAC board, solder bridge pad `SCK` to `GND`. Bridge pads `1`, `2`, and `3` to `GND`. This configures internal PLL clock generation, normal latency filter, and standard I2S format.
  3. Phase 3: Wire 24V Distribution Rails: Strip 11mm of 16 AWG red wire and insert into WAGO 221-415 (+24V). Insert 16 AWG black into WAGO 221-415 (GND). Bridge the 4700µF capacitor legs into Port 1 (+ to Red, - to Black). Snap levers down securely.
  1. Phase 4: Solder Amp Board Leads: Pre-tin 20 AWG wires and solder onto Amp pads 8 (VCC) and 9 (GND). Solder shielded 2-core audio wires onto pads 1 (INL), 2 (GND), 3 (INR). Solder speaker wires onto pads 4-7. Adhere to the 3-second soldering rule!
  2. Phase 5: Digital I2S Bus Harness: Route shared BCK and LRCK DuPont jumpers from ESP32 to all 3 DAC boards and the PCM1808 ADC. Route Pico DOUT lines to DAC #1 (Front) and DAC #3 (Center/Sub). Wire Slave D26/D25 to Pico BCK/LRCK, Slave D35 to PCM1808 OUT, and Slave D27 to DAC #2 (Rear/Surround) DIN.
  3. Phase 6: Multi-Meter Pre-Power Inspection: Check continuity between +24V rail and GND rail (must show high resistance, not a dead short). Check that speaker (-) terminals have infinite resistance to chassis ground. Power ON and verify 5.0V before booting ESP32 firmware!
