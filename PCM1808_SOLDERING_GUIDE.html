What Header Pins Should You Solder?

Your kit includes two 6-pin header strips (plus the 3-pin top analog input).

  • Top 3 Holes (LIN / AGND / RIN): Solder a 3-pin header or solder your shielded 3.5mm Aux cable wires directly into these holes.
  • Right 6 Holes (BCK, OUT, LRC, SCK, GND, 3.3V): Solder the full 6-pin header strip here. You will connect 5 of these to the ESP32.
  • Left 6 Holes (FMT, MD1, MD0, GND, 3.3, +5V): Solder the full 6-pin header strip for mechanical strength, but ONLY wire +5V, 3.3V, and GND. Leave FMT, MD1, and MD0 completely empty!
⚠ IMPORTANT POWER RULE: The PCM1808 chip needs BOTH +5V (for its high-dynamic-range analog input stage) AND 3.3V (for its digital I2S logic stage). Do not skip either power pin!
